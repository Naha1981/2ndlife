    65→## 1. Product Definition
    66→
    67→2ndLife is a multi-tenant SaaS platform that finds revenue already inside a business's systems — inactive, stale, abandoned, unpaid or at-risk — and recovers it through intelligent, empathetic workflows (WhatsApp-first).
    68→
    69→Core question: *"Who already wanted to buy, pay, renew or continue — and what stopped them?"*
    70→
    71→Product loop: **Detect → Prioritize → Engage → Recover → Verify → Measure**
    72→
    73→Initial recovery categories: stale leads, abandoned quotes, unconverted opportunities, lapsed customers, missed renewals, unpaid invoices, failed payments, dormant customers, expired offers, at-risk recurring revenue.
