Businesses don't primarily have a "marketing problem."

They have a **revenue leakage problem**.

Revenue can leak at every stage:

| Stage         | Revenue Leak                                 |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Demand        | Business doesn't know what customers want    |
| Awareness     | Business isn't publishing relevant content   |
| Engagement    | Social questions go unanswered               |
| Lead capture  | Website visitors disappear                   |
| Response      | Leads wait hours for replies                 |
| Qualification | Staff waste time on poor leads               |
| Booking       | Prospects don't commit                       |
| Attendance    | Customers don't show                         |
| Payment       | Customers don't complete payment             |
| Retention     | Customers don't return                       |
| Recovery      | Dormant customers aren't contacted           |
| Attribution   | Business doesn't know what generated revenue |

2ndLife exists to close those leaks.

# 12. Human Approval Policy

The platform supports three modes.

### Mode 1 — Manual

Every post requires approval.

Best for:

* medical
* dental
* legal
* financial services
* regulated businesses

### Mode 2 — Semi-Autonomous

AI generates and schedules within predefined rules.

Human approval required for:

* pricing changes
* medical claims
* legal claims
* promotions
* sensitive subjects

### Mode 3 — Autonomous

AI can publish automatically when:

* content uses approved knowledge
* no restricted claims are detected
* confidence exceeds threshold
* content type is approved
* brand guardrails pass

This is important because **autonomy should be configurable per tenant and per content class.**
